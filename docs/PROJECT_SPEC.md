# Product specification

At 5:00 PM Asia/Kolkata each day, future phases will seek 20–30 newly available fully remote roles aligned to the canonical profile, favoring small startups and growing technology companies when enough roles exist. Official job applications remain manual.

Every qualifying job and official portal will be saved to the CRM. Public evidence-backed contacts may later receive Gemini-personalized initial outreach on Day 0, then follow-ups on Day 3 and Day 6. The sequence stops after reply, bounce, opt-out, suppression, manual pause, closed role, or the second follow-up. Jobs with no safely usable email remain in the CRM and are not auto-contacted.

Phase 1 is foundation-only: no live job discovery, crawling, Gmail activity, or production automation.

Phase 3 adds public read-only Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby job ingestion, source health, canonical normalization, conservative job lifecycle tracking, and manual official applications. Eligibility scoring, Gemini assessment, contact discovery, and outreach remain disabled until later phases.
